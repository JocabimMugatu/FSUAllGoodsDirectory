Accessibility enhancements

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation with visible focus rings and logical tab order across all interactive elements.
  • Reduced motion preference respected by disabling transitions for users who opt out of animation.
  • Theme toggle communicates state with aria-pressed and preserves preferences between visits.
  • Semantic regions, headings, and skip navigation support screen-reader comprehension.
